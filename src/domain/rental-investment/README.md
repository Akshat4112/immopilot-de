# Rental investment (CF-010)

`calculateRentalInvestment` implements PD-007 §10 against the existing CF-003 financing result and CF-005/006 month-by-month amortization result. It does **not** construct a second mortgage schedule. Its PD-010 Baden-Württemberg fixture tests yield, pre-tax cash flow, debt and an illustrative sale to the cent. Inputs and output amounts are integer cents; rates are decimal annual rates or fractions, not percentages (e.g. 0.05 means 5%).

## Accounting contract

- Annual cold rent is 12 times monthly Nettokaltmiete. Effective annual rent is annual cold rent after the editable vacancy fraction, less other annual rent loss. Combined loss must not exceed available rent; recoverable tenant-paid charges do not enter either side.
- Owner costs are twelve times non-recoverable Hausgeld **excluding reserves**, plus twelve times the separately entered reserve, plus annual maintenance **outside** Hausgeld and other annual costs. The categories are mutually exclusive: if Hausgeld already contains a reserve or an entered maintenance allowance, correct the inputs to avoid double counting. Vacancy carrying costs can be entered in other annual owner costs. Neither the engine nor a user-entered amount can verify the accounting documents automatically.
- Gross yield = annual cold rent / purchase price; net yield = (effective annual rent − owner costs) / full CF-003 project-cost basis. Both numerators and denominators are exposed. These yields exclude financing and taxes.
- Each projected monthly effective rent and owner cost grows using the section 9 effective monthly factor `(1 + annualRate)^(1/12)`, starting from annual effective amounts divided by 12 and independently rounded half-up to cents. Monthly cash flow before extra = effective rent − owner costs − the **actual regular mortgage payment**; after extra additionally subtracts the actual Sondertilgung. Regular mortgage payment and debt become zero after payoff. First-year annual cash flow is the sum of the twelve rounded monthly figures, regardless of a shorter holding period. The cash-on-cash numerator is **before** discretionary Sondertilgung; if required equity is zero the yield and cash flow remain available while cash-on-cash has its own typed `ZERO_REQUIRED_EQUITY` result.
- Holding-period debt reduction = starting loan − actual closing balance after the holding month. The optional sale requires **both** property appreciation and selling-cost assumptions; omitting both returns `not-requested`. Projected sale price is independently rounded to cents, then selling costs are rounded from that displayed sale price. Net proceeds = rounded sale price − rounded selling costs − debt. Illustrative pre-tax profit = net proceeds + cumulative **after-extra** cash flow − required equity. Principal repayment is already in remaining debt and must not be added again to profit.

The available result exposes `rows` through at least month 12 (or the holding period if longer), an explicit sale status, the yield bases, cash-on-cash status, and mortgage projection labeling. Horizon must be 1–1,200 whole months. Funding gaps, unavailable upstream calculations, mismatched loan principal, or a missing schedule row before payoff are typed unavailable results. Invalid money, vacancy, loss, growth, sale rates or missing paired sale assumptions return stable field-specific validation errors. A schedule beyond **Zinsbindung** at the initial rate is only a projection; if the loan was already repaid by the end of Zinsbindung, the output does not claim a post-period mortgage-rate projection.

## Limits

This is an editable educational **pre-tax** scenario, not investment, tax or legal advice, a property valuation or an offer. Excluded: income and capital-gains tax, AfA and mortgage-interest deductions, early-repayment compensation, refinancing and guarantee fees, inflation, actual rent restrictions, future repair shocks, property market risk and tenant-specific payment failures. The vacancy fraction and flat other rent loss are illustrative expected-income assumptions; calendar-month vacancies and time-varying rent restrictions are not simulated. Carrying costs during vacancy, additional sale costs and financing beyond Zinsbindung are included only if explicitly modeled. The projected sale and profit do not guarantee realizable prices or returns. Verify Hausgeld recoverability, reserve treatment, maintenance, taxes and legal constraints with qualified professionals for a real purchase.
