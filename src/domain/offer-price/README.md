# Offer-price calculation (CF-011)

`calculateOfferPrice` implements PD-007 §11 using the CF-002 **individually rounded** acquisition calculator, CF-003 available-equity financing and CF-004 German initial-repayment mortgage payment. The rental gross and net numerator amounts come from an available CF-010 rental result for the **same property**. Targets and assumptions are user-selected decimal rates, not platform forecasts or negotiation advice. The acquisition template must have confirmed renovation and moving/setup budgets; the search changes **only** the purchase price, never the Bundesland, broker involvement, assumptions or other scenario inputs.

## Ceilings

- Gross yield ceiling uses annual Nettokaltmiete divided by the user-selected **positive** gross-yield target; its denominator is purchase price alone, so acquisition-cost rounding is irrelevant. It is floored to integer cents.
- Net yield analytical ceiling is `(NOI / target − fixed renovation/setup budget) / (1 + proportional acquisition rates)`. The result is refined by a full monotone binary search that calls CF-002 at each candidate. Its verified ceiling uses `NOI / (candidate price + individually rounded transaction costs + fixed budget) ≥ target`, and the next cent fails. A negative analytical ceiling is unavailable, not an invented positive price.
- Affordability takes editable available equity, financed acquisition-cost share, nominal interest, initial repayment and **maximum monthly payment**. It computes analytical loan capacity and both payment and cash-cost limits, then searches exact candidate prices with CF-002, CF-003 `available-equity` mode and CF-004. A candidate requires funded cash costs and a valid rounded monthly payment within the limit; the next cent fails. All available equity may be used. Zero is a valid mathematical ceiling meaning **no positive purchase price is feasible**, never an actual purchase. Fixed costs greater than available equity are unavailable-underfunded. The cash-cost limit may be unbounded when cash-funded proportional fees are zero; this does not imply unlimited payment affordability.

The search covers prices from €0.01 through **€10 billion** (`maximumOfferPriceSearchCents = 1e12`). If a candidate at the search limit is still feasible, it returns typed `PRICE_SEARCH_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` rather than falsely claiming that is the highest price. A handful of nearby cents is insufficient for payment rounding: one rounded monthly-payment cent can correspond to many price cents. The exact search tests the whole interval and validates the next-cent boundary. Inputs above safe integer-cent bounds or impossible arithmetic yield stable validation errors. The rental result's purchase-price and full project-cost bases must match the acquisition template; mismatched or unavailable rental inputs affect only the requested yield ceilings, not affordability or comparables.

## Comparables and negotiation

`livingAreaSquareMetres × user-entered low/high €/m²` provides an independently half-up-rounded **comparison range**, not a certified `Verkehrswertgutachten`. The user-selected purchase offer minus asking price is exposed in integer cents and as a signed decimal rate. An opening-offer range is **not requested by default**: it requires explicit reference price and larger/smaller discounts (`0 ≤ smaller ≤ larger < 1`). Prices at each discount are rounded half-up to cents. The range does not infer a fair offer or seller willingness.

## Limitations

Educational scenario limits, not a financing offer, lender approval, professional property valuation or legal/tax/investment advice. Lender fees, mortgage eligibility and reserves, risk of future interest rates, taxes, legal rent limits, valuation uncertainty, sale costs and transaction timing are omitted unless separately modeled. Yield ceilings hold the current rent, vacancy, operating costs and fixed budgets unchanged when price varies; financing ceiling assumes the **initial-rate** payment mode and all available equity, not a fixed down-payment amount. Verify effective Kaltmiete, non-recoverable Hausgeld, renovation scope, broker contract and feasible borrowing terms before making any real offer.
